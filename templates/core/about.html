About Us

Welcome to newmovies — your go-to destination for discovering and streaming movies and TV series.

Our Mission

We aim to provide a seamless, ad-free browsing experience for movie and TV series enthusiasts. Our platform aggregates content from various sources to help you find what to watch next.

What We Offer

  • Curated lists of popular, top-rated, and upcoming movies and TV series
  • Detailed information powered by TMDB (The Movie Database)
  • Multiple streaming servers for flexible playback options
  • Personal watchlist to keep track of what you want to watch
  • Genre and country-based filtering for easy discovery

Content Sources

All movie and TV show data, including descriptions, ratings, cast information, and images, is provided by TMDB. We do not host or store any video content on our servers.
