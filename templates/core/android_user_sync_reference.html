8. Important Notes
  • The idToken should be verified server-side via Google's OAuth2 tokeninfo endpoint.
  • The deviceId should be used to track unique device connections per user.
  • Subscription status should be checked against your database, not hardcoded.
  • The timestamp field helps detect clock skew and replay attacks.
  • Rate-limit this endpoint to prevent abuse from repeated login attempts.
