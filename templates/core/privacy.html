Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how newmovies ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP Address: Used for analytics and to prevent abuse. Stored temporarily and not linked to personal identity.
  • Browser & Device Info: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution for optimizing the experience.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns — used only for improving the site.

Information You Provide

  • Email Address: Only if you subscribe to our mailing list. Used solely for sending updates.
  • Account Information: If you create an account, we store your email and watchlist data.

2. Cookies & Advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site functionality (login, preferences).
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how visitors use our site.
  • Advertising Cookies: Third-party ad partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads. These partners have their own privacy policies.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Services

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): We use TMDB's API for movie and TV show data. TMDB has its own privacy policy.
  • Advertising Partners: We may display ads from third-party networks. These partners may collect data per their own policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data that cannot identify you individually.

5. Data Retention

  • Analytics data: Retained for up to 30 days (configurable by admin).
  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account.
  • Subscription data: Retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
  • Opt out of advertising cookies via browser settings

7. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at alok.ak639@gmail.com or visit our Contact Us page.
